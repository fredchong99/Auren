**AUREN as an AI Rehearsal Platform.**

*Teaching retail investors not just what to know — but how to respond when it matters most.*

This paper reimagines AUREN — the Investor Intelligence Academy submitted to the IOSCO TechSprint 2026 — as an AI-powered rehearsal environment where retail investors practice, in safe simulation, the exact situations that will define their real-world outcomes: an AI tool giving them dubious investment advice; a deepfake video of a trusted figure endorsing a suspicious platform; a WhatsApp contact pressuring them to act fast; a market panic tempting them to sell everything.

The v1 AUREN taught concepts. The v2 AUREN — described in this paper — puts learners inside the moment. Every rehearsal is scored with quote-level evidence. Every session produces measurable competency data. Every learner emerges with skills that can be certified, not just assumed.

This is what changes when AUREN adopts the architectural pattern used by leading AI rehearsal platforms in enterprise training — and applies it to the one context IOSCO has identified as most urgent: investor protection in the AI era.

**1. The observation that changed the design.**

Enterprise sales teams, insurance advisors, and customer-service organizations have solved a problem that investor education has not: they have moved from lecture-based training to simulation-based rehearsal. When an insurance advisor trains today, they do not sit through a slideshow on risk disclosure — they role-play a live consultation with an AI persona that hesitates, pushes back, and probes for weaknesses. The system scores their conversation against specific competencies, flags risky phrasing in real time, and produces evidence-based certification.

This model works because it treats skill acquisition the way skill acquisition actually works: through practice under conditions that resemble the real event, with immediate feedback and measurable outcomes.

Retail investors face conversations too — but nobody trains them for those conversations. Nobody rehearses with them what to say when a friend pushes them to invest in a trending crypto platform. Nobody role-plays with them how to respond when an AI chatbot tells them a fund is "guaranteed to outperform." Nobody drills them on the exact moment they encounter a deepfake video of a famous CEO endorsing an investment scheme. Yet these are the situations that determine whether retail investors lose their savings or protect them.

**2. What AUREN v2 is.**

AUREN v2 is an AI rehearsal platform for retail investors. It combines four operating elements — none of which exist together in any current investor education product.

**2.1 A Digital Human host who leads the learning journey.**

A hyper-realistic AI Digital Human is present at every step — conducting the onboarding interview, walking the learner through simulated scenarios, providing live coaching during rehearsals, delivering scorecard feedback, and adapting the course path in real time. She is not a mascot. She is the mentor the learner would have if every investor had access to a full-time financial educator.

**2.2 A rehearsal engine that puts the learner inside high-stakes scenarios.**

The core learning mechanic is not video, not quiz, not reading. It is live rehearsal. The learner enters a scenario, converses with an AI persona (a scam recruiter, a pressuring friend, a misleading AI chatbot, a panicking market voice), and their words, hesitations, questions, and decisions are captured, analyzed, and scored. The AI persona hesitates, applies pressure, invokes urgency, and probes for the exact behavioral vulnerabilities documented in retail-investor fraud research.

**2.3 An evidential scorecard system with quote-level feedback.**

Every rehearsal produces a scorecard with granular metrics. Not just "you scored 42" — but "at turn 3 you said 'sure, sounds legit' when the AI persona pressured you; here is why that is a scam-vulnerability signal, and here is the response AUREN teaches instead." Scoring uses the actual transcript as evidence. Learners see exactly what they did well, what they missed, and what the right response looks like — anchored in their own words.

**2.4 A certification and dashboard system for individuals, institutions, and regulators.**

Sessions are auto-classified as certified, needs-review, or requires-retraining. A learner dashboard tracks personal progress across competencies. An institutional dashboard lets banks, universities, and employers see the readiness of their populations. A regulator dashboard aggregates anonymized cohort data — showing which behavioral vulnerabilities are most prevalent in a jurisdiction and where public literacy is improving.

**3. The operating flow: seven steps from onboarding to certification.**

AUREN v2 follows a single, repeatable flow. Every learner experiences the same architecture, but the content is personalized end-to-end.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **01** | **Onboarding + Identity Verification**  The learner signs up, verifies identity through integrated eKYC (passport, national ID, or QR-to-phone), and completes a brief profile. The credential this journey produces is trustworthy from day one. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **02** | **Digital Human Interview**  AUREN conducts a 15-minute conversational interview — not a questionnaire. She observes reasoning patterns, AI trust levels, risk biases, emotional response indicators, and conversational tells. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **03** | **AILS Cognitive Assessment**  The AI cognitive engine generates the learner's starting AILS score across five dimensions (Investor Reasoning, AI Literacy, Risk Awareness, Scam Resistance, Behavioral Stability). Score comes with quantitative bars, qualitative narrative, named strengths, and specific improvement areas. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **04** | **Personalized Rehearsal Path**  Based on the AILS profile, AUREN generates a personalized sequence of rehearsal scenarios. Weak dimensions get more time. Strong dimensions get lighter coverage. Every scenario is chosen to strengthen a specific competency. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **05** | **Rehearsal Sessions with Live Coaching**  The learner enters each scenario. AUREN speaks first — sometimes as herself the coach, sometimes as the AI persona in role. Live coaching prompts appear when the learner uses risky phrasing, misses a verification step, or shows behavioral signals of pressure or panic. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **06** | **Evidential Scorecard**  After each rehearsal, the learner sees a scorecard with quote-level evidence. What they said, when they said it, why it mattered, what the alternative response would have been, and how it affected their competency scores. |

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **07** | **Certification, Retraining, or Escalation**  Sessions are auto-classified. Certified scenarios move the learner forward. Retrain classifications trigger a repeat with variations. Escalations flag the learner for review — either self-directed remediation or, in institutional deployments, human review by a supervising educator. |

**4. The six rehearsal categories.**

AUREN v2 organizes its rehearsal content into six categories. Each addresses a specific vulnerability documented in retail-investor fraud research and each maps to at least one of IOSCO's problem statements.

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deepfake & Voice Clone Recognition**  Learners rehearse detecting AI-generated video and audio impersonations of trusted figures. Live coaching walks them through the specific signals — lip-sync mismatch, unnatural blink rate, audio artifacts. | **AI Tool Literacy**  Learners rehearse dialogues with AI chatbots giving misleading, hallucinated, or advisory-toned investment content. They practice verification workflows, source-checking, and knowing when to escalate. | **Social Media Pressure**  Learners rehearse the exact dynamics of WhatsApp investment groups, TikTok finfluencer promotions, and Telegram pump-and-dump rings. They practice recognizing scam patterns and resisting pressure. |

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Emotional Investing Discipline**  Learners rehearse market-crash panic, FOMO-driven hype cycles, and loss-aversion moments. They practice the discipline of returning to a written plan rather than reacting emotionally. | **Product Comprehension**  Learners rehearse conversations where the AI persona pressures them to invest in complex products (structured notes, leveraged ETFs, crypto derivatives). They practice asking the questions that reveal actual risk. | **Advice vs Education Boundary**  Learners rehearse recognizing when they need a licensed professional and when general education is enough. They practice the escalation moment — knowing what they don't know. |

*Each category uses the same AI rehearsal engine — only the scenarios, AI personas, and scoring criteria change. Adding new categories, new jurisdictions, or new scam typologies does not require rebuilding the platform.*

**5. What changed from v1 to v2.**

AUREN v1 was already a strong submission for the IOSCO TechSprint. It combined a Digital Human host, an AI cognitive assessment engine, a 3-day adaptive course, a Spot-the-Scam Lab, and a regulator dashboard. v2 keeps all of those components — but restructures the learning mechanic around rehearsal instead of lecture.

The difference is not additive. It is architectural.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **AUREN v1**  **•** Learner consumes content, then answers questions  **•** Course completion measured by module count  **•** Assessment happens at start and end  **•** Feedback is post-hoc — after the module ends  **•** Certification means the learner finished the course  **•** Scam awareness taught through explanation | **AUREN v2**  **•** Learner rehearses the exact situations that matter  **•** Course completion measured by demonstrated competency  **•** Assessment happens continuously, every session  **•** Feedback is live — during the rehearsal, in the moment  **•** Certification means the learner demonstrated skill  **•** Scam resistance built through repeated rehearsal |

The change reflects a broader principle. Knowledge does not survive contact with pressure. Skill does. AUREN v1 delivered knowledge well. AUREN v2 delivers skill.

**6. How v2 strengthens AUREN's answer to IOSCO.**

The IOSCO TechSprint is judged on five weighted criteria. AUREN v2 strengthens the answer to all five — but the largest gains are in Consumer Impact and Feasibility & Team Capability, which together account for 45% of the score.

**Clarity & Relevance (25%)**

v2 answers both IOSCO problem statements more directly. PS1 (AI-enabled fraud) is not addressed only through scam-pattern education — it is addressed through repeated rehearsal in scenarios that mirror the exact fraud environments the learner will encounter. PS2 (AI literacy for capital markets) is not taught only through modules — it is practiced through simulated dialogues with AI tools that hallucinate, mislead, or blur the education-vs-advice line.

**Consumer Impact (25%)**

v2 produces measurable behavioral change, not just measurable content consumption. The evidential scorecard system means every learner can be shown exactly which competencies they have built and which they still need to strengthen. Behavioral change is what actually protects investors — and AUREN v2 is one of very few platforms that can prove it.

**Feasibility & Team Capability (20%)**

The rehearsal architecture — scenario, AI persona, live coaching, evidential scorecard, dashboard — is a proven pattern already operating at scale in enterprise training environments. AUREN v2 does not require inventing new architecture. It applies a validated architecture to a new and urgent context. This lowers execution risk substantially.

**Innovation & Uniqueness (10%)**

No existing investor education product uses live rehearsal with evidential scoring. Existing platforms range from static courses (Investopedia, Khan Academy) to gamified content (finance apps). None simulate the actual conversations investors will have with AI tools, scam vectors, and social pressure. AUREN v2 is the first.

**Cross-Jurisdictional Potential (20%)**

The rehearsal architecture is inherently jurisdiction-neutral. Scenarios and AI personas can be localized — a UK deepfake scenario looks different from a Brazilian one, and both are different from a Malaysian pump-and-dump WhatsApp group. Each jurisdiction gets its own scenario library, its own AI persona voices, its own regulator dashboard framing. One platform. Any jurisdiction.

**7. Deployment models unlocked by v2.**

The rehearsal architecture creates new commercial pathways that were not available to v1.

* **Retail investors (B2C).** Subscription access to rehearsal library, AILS assessment, and verified credential. Freemium tier with premium unlock for advanced scenarios and certification.
* **Banks and brokers (B2B).** Onboarding-integrated rehearsal for new retail customers, particularly high-risk products. Institutional dashboard for compliance officers. Reduces mis-selling exposure by measurably improving customer comprehension.
* **Universities and academic institutions.** Embedded rehearsal curriculum for finance, business, and AI literacy programs. Student cohort dashboards. Verified credential adds market value to graduating students.
* **Regulators (IOSCO members).** National public literacy programs. Cross-jurisdictional cohort analytics. Rehearsal libraries updated with each regulator's own scam typologies and consumer warnings. Regulator dashboard provides real-time visibility into population-level vulnerability shifts.
* **Corporate compliance and financial-services training.** Adapted rehearsal libraries for financial advisor training, compliance certification, and continuing education requirements. Same engine, different scenarios, different scoring criteria.

The rehearsal architecture makes AUREN horizontally applicable across every context where retail investors encounter risk. It also makes AUREN vertically extensible — the same engine, retuned for advisor training, becomes a compliance product for financial institutions. The commercial optionality is significantly greater than v1.

**8. What we are asking IOSCO to see.**

On Demo Day, October 8, 2026, in Madrid, AUREN will be one of a small number of submissions presented to a judging panel of 39 C8 Plenary members and the IOSCO Secretary General. Most submissions will be prototype education tools. What AUREN will show is different.

AUREN will show a live rehearsal. A learner will enter a scenario where a deepfake video is trying to convince them to invest in a fraudulent scheme. The AI persona will pressure them. AUREN the Digital Human will coach them live. The scorecard will appear at the end — quote-level, evidence-based, competency-mapped. The regulator dashboard view will show what a national deployment would look like.

The judges will not be watching a demo. They will be watching what investor education can become when the mechanic changes — when knowledge delivery gives way to skill rehearsal, when scoring becomes evidential, when the AI takes the role of both mentor and adversary in service of the learner's protection.

**9. In closing.**

The best investor protection is not a warning label. It is a trained instinct. AUREN v1 was an academy. AUREN v2 is a rehearsal ground — and a rehearsal ground is what the AI era demands, because the AI era does not give retail investors time to look things up, verify sources, or reflect on best practices. It gives them a moment. AUREN v2 trains them for that moment.
