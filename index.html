Five compounding vulnerabilities — each one amplified by AI

Vulnerability · 1 of 5

Information asymmetry, widened by AI

“Generative AI floods the information space with content that mimics authentic financial communication: fabricated research reports, synthetic news articles, cloned analyst commentary.”

Vulnerability · 2 of 5

Social media as the primary vector

“Investment decisions are increasingly mediated by TikTok, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Discord — environments optimized for virality rather than verification.”

Vulnerability · 3 of 5

Behavioral vulnerability targeted at scale

“What a skilled con artist once did to one victim at a time, AI now does to millions in minutes, with each attempt automatically tuned to its target.”

Vulnerability · 4 of 5

Overreliance on AI-generated outputs

“Many investors treat AI responses as authoritative analysis... The interface conveys confidence; the underlying epistemics often do not warrant it.”

Vulnerability · 5 of 5

Hallucination presented with certainty

“A hallucinated statistic delivered with the fluency of a trusted advisor can drive a real loss.”

The seven-step operating flow

“One repeatable architecture. Every learner. Personalized end-to-end.” — Project Paper Deck · Slide 14

Step 01eKYC Onboarding

“Passport, ID, or QR-to-phone. The credential is trustworthy from day one.”

Step 02Digital Human Interview

“A 15-minute conversation. Not a questionnaire. AUREN observes reasoning, biases, tells.”

Step 03AILS Assessment

“Starting score across five dimensions with narrative, strengths, improvement areas.”

Step 04Personalized Path

“Scenarios sequenced against the learner's weakest dimensions.”

Step 05 ⟲Rehearsal + Live Coaching

“Coaching prompts appear when phrasing is risky, verification is skipped, or pressure shows.”

Step 06 ⟲Evidential Scorecard

“Quote-level feedback. What they said, when, why it mattered, what to say instead.”

Step 07 ⟲Certify · Retrain · Escalate

“Retrain triggers a variation, so the learner cannot pass by memorizing a script.”

“Steps 05–07 loop across the personalized rehearsal path until competency is demonstrated... it is a training cycle that repeats until the skill is real.”

What We Are Asking IOSCO to See

The best investor protection is not a warning label. It is a trained instinct.

The AI era does not give retail investors time to look things up, verify sources, or reflect on best practices when it matters. It gives them a moment — a call that sounds exactly like their advisor, a video that looks exactly like a trusted CEO, a message from a friend that carries exactly the right social proof. AUREN trains them for that moment, before it happens, so that when it happens, they are ready.
